What we do

Two things.
Done well.

Most firms stop at the deck. We don't. One half of our work is strategy and operations. The other half is the software that makes the strategy real. The two halves talk to each other, which is the whole point.

01 · Consult

Operators who've built and sold businesses, sitting next to you in the weeds.

  • Operations audits.Find the leaks and the levers.
  • Growth strategy.Positioning, pricing, channels.
  • Systems design.Tools, workflows, roles, handoffs.
  • Fractional leadership.Step in as COO, Head of Ops, Head of Tech.
  • Owner transition.Prep the business to run without you.

02 · Build

In house engineering team that ships the software the strategy requires.

  • Internal tools.Dashboards, admin apps, back offices.
  • Custom web & mobile apps.From first screen to launch.
  • Integrations.ERP, accounting, ecommerce, POS, 3PL.
  • AI & automations.Agents and workflows that remove the busywork.
  • Venture MVPs.Brand new products, built to defend a lead.
How we work

A short road.
Walked together.

No ninety page deck no one reads. No six month "discovery." We move in weeks, not quarters, and the work ships as we go. You stay close to it the whole time.

01

Diagnose

Two weeks with your team, your numbers, and your tools. We leave with a short, honest list of what's working and what isn't.

02

Decide

One strategy document. One roadmap. The handful of moves worth making this quarter, and the ones to kill.

03

Deliver

We build. In sprints. In your Slack. Live software your team can use before the quarter is out.

04

Operate

We train your team, hand off the keys, and stay on retainer for the next thing. The engagement ends when you're ready, not when the SOW does.

How we think

A short list
of things we believe.

What you hire when you hire us.

01
Build the thing, don't describe it.

A working prototype beats a hundred slide deck. The sooner your team can click something, the sooner we know what's wrong.

02
Operators, not analysts.

Everyone on our team has run a P&L, shipped software to real customers, or both. We've made the mistakes we're helping you avoid.

03
Own the whole chain.

Strategy without execution is wasted. Execution without strategy is expensive. We refuse to do one half without the other.

04
Short engagements, long relationships.

We'd rather be your call in year three than your invoice in year one. Most of our clients are referred by other clients.

05
Price the work, not the hours.

Fixed scope and fixed price for strategy and MVPs. Retainers for ongoing partnership. No hourly billing.

06
Leave it running.

We hand off clean code, trained teams, and documentation. If we vanished tomorrow, the business would keep humming.
